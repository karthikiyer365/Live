What was run · Model D

Fine-tune Laya on the training rows

Started from Laya's official Apple-Silicon fine-tune script and changed only what the evaluation rules, or a failure, required.

Official script doesModel D does insteadWhy
Trains on its own benchmark datasetOur 11,670 training rows from splits.csv, raw text, same typed question as Model BSame rows and question as every other model
Calibrates on a random 10% of training rowsTrains on all training rows; calibrates on validation logitsA random slice holds reworded siblings of training tickets: a leak
Saves one checkpoint, never scores validationSaves every epoch and scores validation after eachThe epoch is chosen on validation, never on test
Starts at full learning rate, adds an RL term to the loss10% linear warmup, cross-entropy only (RL off)Seed 42 diverged without warmup, and again with it while the RL term was on
No stop ruleAborts on NaN or when loss doublesA bad run dies in the first hour, not after eight

Find why training diverged

done

Warmup alone was not enough. One change at a time: no gradient checkpointing, 0.3× learning rate, and RL term off. Only RL off kept the loss falling past 2,400 tickets, so the final recipe is warmup plus cross-entropy only. Logs: logs/diag_*.log.
